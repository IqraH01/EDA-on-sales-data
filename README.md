<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>
An exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the Global Superstore sales dataset, looking at how sales and profit vary across products, regions and categories, and how sales change over time.
</p>
<h2>Questions Explored</h2>
<ul>
    <li>What are the total sales and total profit?</li>
    <li>Which products sell the most?</li>
    <li>How do sales differ by region and product category?</li>
    <li>How have monthly sales changed over time?</li>
    <li>Is there a relationship between sales and profit?</li>
</ul>
<h2>Methods</h2>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Data cleaning:</strong> removed missing values and converted order and ship dates to datetime format</li>
    <li><strong>Aggregation:</strong> grouped sales by product, region and month</li>
    <li><strong>Correlation:</strong> measured the relationship between sales and profit</li>
    <li><strong>Visualisation:</strong> bar chart, line graph, scatter plot and heatmap</li>
</ul>
